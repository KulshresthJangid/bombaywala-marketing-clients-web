Data Deletion

Effective date: November 20, 2026

You can ask Zoralis to delete the data we hold about you or your business at any time. This page explains how, what is deleted, and how to disconnect the channels you linked to Zoralis. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

1. How to request deletion

Email bombaywalamarketing@gmail.com with the subject "Zoralis: Delete my data", sent from the email address on your Zoralis account. Please include:

  • Your name and your business name
  • The channels you connected (for example the WhatsApp number, Instagram account or website)
  • Whether you want everything deleted or only specific data

We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act, so that nobody can delete an account that isn't theirs.

2. What we delete

  • Your account details, such as your email address
  • Connected channels and the access tokens we stored for them (WhatsApp, Instagram, website)
  • Conversation history: the messages exchanged with your customers on the channels you connected
  • Documents, FAQs and flow descriptions you added to Zoralis

3. What we may keep

We may keep limited records that we are legally required to keep, or that we need to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not use them for anything else.

4. How long it takes

We aim to complete a verified request within 30 days and will email you when it is done.

5. Disconnect your channels yourself

You can cut off Zoralis's access at any time without waiting for us. For WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook, open your Meta Business settings and remove the Zoralis app from your connected integrations. For your website, remove the Zoralis widget code. Once access is removed, Zoralis can no longer read new messages from that channel.

6. Data sent to AI providers

If you chose an AI provider (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or your own endpoint), messages and document content needed for a reply are sent to that provider and are governed by its own terms. We cannot delete data held by a provider you chose; to remove it, contact that provider directly.

7. Contact

Questions about deletion can be sent to bombaywalamarketing@gmail.com.
