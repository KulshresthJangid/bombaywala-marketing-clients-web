Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Zoralis ("Zoralis", "we", "us") is a conversation automation platform that lets a business describe a flow in plain English and run it across WhatsApp, Instagram and a website, answering from the business's own documents and FAQs. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — including while Zoralis is in early access.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us directly — such as your email address and anything else you include when you request early access or contact us.

Usage information — standard technical data collected automatically when you visit this site, such as approximate location from IP address, browser and device type, and which pages you viewed.

Content you connect to the product — once you're using Zoralis, this can include documents, FAQs, flow descriptions, and the messages exchanged with your customers on the channels you connect (WhatsApp, Instagram, your website).

2. How we use information

  • To respond to early-access requests and set up your account
  • To operate, maintain, debug and improve the product
  • To send you product updates, but only if you've asked to receive them

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. AI processing and third-party providers

Zoralis lets you choose which AI model powers your replies — currently OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, a self-hosted Ollama instance, or a custom HTTP endpoint you configure. When a conversation needs an AI-generated reply, the relevant message and any connected document or FAQ content is sent to the provider you've chosen to generate that reply. We don't route your content to a provider you haven't connected, and each provider's own terms and privacy policy govern how they handle data you send them.

4. Data retention

We keep account and conversation data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your data at any time using the contact details below.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and as an early-access product, Zoralis is still actively being built and hardened.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email us at bombaywalamarketing@gmail.com.

7. Children's privacy

Zoralis is intended for businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

8. Cookies

This site uses only the cookies or local storage needed for basic functionality. We don't run third-party advertising trackers.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Zoralis moves from early access toward general availability. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to bombaywalamarketing@gmail.com.
