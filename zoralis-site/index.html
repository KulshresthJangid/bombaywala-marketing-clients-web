FAQ

Questions teams ask before they start.

What is Zoralis?

Zoralis is a conversation automation platform. You build one visual flow that runs across WhatsApp, Instagram and your website, and it answers customers using your own documents and FAQs.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Flows are built in a no-code visual builder with conditions, AI replies, buttons and handoff. Developers can plug in custom endpoints when they want to.

Which AI models can I use?

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, a self-hosted Ollama model, or any custom HTTP endpoint. You can use different models for different flows.

Can agencies manage multiple clients?

Yes. Each customer gets an isolated organization with its own workspaces, and you can switch between client workspaces from a single login.

Is it secure enough for enterprise teams?

Zoralis supports single sign-on via OAuth2 / OpenID Connect, plus organizations, workspaces and role-based permissions. You can also route sensitive content to the AI provider that fits your compliance needs.
