Part one — the problem

Most teams don't have one automation problem. They have four.

Before looking at how Zoralis works, it's worth being specific about what actually breaks when a growing business tries to automate conversations with tools built for something smaller.

1

Fragmented toolchain

Separate tools for the chatbot, the knowledge base, approvals, and channel connections force constant context-switching. Every new channel means another login, another integration, another place for a conversation to get lost.

2

Chatbots that don't know your business

Most AI chatbots reply from a blank prompt, with no memory of your docs, your FAQs, or what's already been answered before. Every conversation starts from zero, so the bot keeps giving generic answers to specific questions.

3

Agencies can't scale safely

Managing dozens of client brands from one login, with proper isolation and access control, is an afterthought bolted onto consumer tools. One shared password, one shared workspace, one mistake away from the wrong client seeing the wrong data.

4

Enterprise adoption stalls at IT

Security and compliance teams block automation tools that lack real single sign-on, roles, and audit-grade access control. The pilot that worked great for marketing never makes it past procurement.
