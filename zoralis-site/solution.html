Part two — the solution

One flow that connects your channels, your content, and your team.

Each of the four problems on the last page maps to something Zoralis is specifically built to fix. Here's the walkthrough, step by step — then an honest look at what's live today versus still being built.

Step by step

From a message to a resolved conversation

1

Connect a channel

Securely link WhatsApp or Instagram, or embed the website widget. One connection, not a separate integration for each.

2

Build a knowledge bucket

Add source material: docs, FAQs, past conversations, notes. This is the fix for chatbots that don't know your business.

3

AI processing

Content is summarized and indexed into a searchable knowledge base, so the flow has something real to draw on.

4

Design a flow

Visual builder: conditions, AI replies, quick replies, buttons, catalog messages. No separate tool for logic versus content.

5

AI responds

The flow answers automatically, using the AI provider your workspace configures — not a fixed, one-size model.

6

Human handoff

Pause and notify a real teammate the moment a conversation needs one. Automation that knows its own limits.

7

Resolve

The conversation completes, tagged and logged for the next one — so patterns show up instead of disappearing.
